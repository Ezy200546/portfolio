Put your real profile photo here as:

    profile.jpg

Recommended: a square-ish photo, at least 500x500px, reasonably well-lit.

The homepage currently points to a placeholder graphic (profile-placeholder.svg)
so the site works before you add a real photo. Once you add profile.jpg,
open index.html, find the <img id="profile-photo" ...> tag in the hero
section, and change its src from:

    assets/images/profile-placeholder.svg

to:

    assets/images/profile.jpg

That's the only change needed.
